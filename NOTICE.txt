Kokoro for Core ML, as LuceVoce uses it on the Neural Engine of a Mac, an iPhone or an iPad.

The seven models in this archive are Kokoro-82M (hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M,
Apache License 2.0) converted to Core ML with laishere/kokoro-coreml
(https://github.com/laishere/kokoro-coreml, commit 484907db6a8347a6afb6e7b86850ea2878c6a3fb, Apache License 2.0)
and compiled with Xcode's coremlcompiler. LuceVoce's script for it: tools/kokoro_coreml/convert.sh.

Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

Kokoro for ONNX Runtime (release kokoro-onnx-1: kokoro-v1.0.onnx and voices-v1.0.bin), as LuceVoce uses it on
every platform.

The two files are Kokoro-82M (hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, Apache License 2.0)
converted to ONNX, with its voices packed into one file, by thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx
(https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx, release model-files-v1.0; the project's code is under the MIT
License, the model under the Apache License 2.0). They are copied from that release unchanged.

Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

Piper voices (release piper-1: fr_FR-mls-medium, it_IT-serena-high, es_ES-carlfm-x_low), as LuceVoce offers them for
French, Italian and Spanish.

Copied unchanged from rhasspy/piper-voices (https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices, MIT License, Copyright (c)
2022 Michael Hansen). fr_FR-mls-medium: trained on Multilingual LibriSpeech, French (https://openslr.org/94/, Pratap
and others, 2020), CC BY 4.0. it_IT-serena-high: trained on serena-synthetic-it-27h by Marco Baccarani
(https://huggingface.co/datasets/committa/serena-synthetic-it-27h), CC BY 4.0, made with Qwen3-TTS (Apache 2.0) and
sentences adapted from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org, CC BY 2.0 FR). es_ES-carlfm-x_low: trained on
carlfm01/my-speech-datasets, public domain. CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
uk_UA-ukrainian_tts-medium (added 19 Nov 2026, from revision 42301ec547bc2939386cf0ae9f9050ca05b1d3af): trained on the
Lada, Mykyta and Tetiana recordings of Open Source Ukrainian Text-to-Speech datasets by Yehor Smoliakov
(https://github.com/egorsmkv/ukrainian-tts-datasets; texts https://github.com/egorsmkv/uk-tts-dataset-text), Apache
License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0); its model card names CC0 data. No changes made.

Whisper speech models (release whisper-ggml-1: ggml-base.bin, ggml-small.bin, ggml-medium.bin), as LuceVoce on
Windows offers them for voice notes.

OpenAI Whisper (https://github.com/openai/whisper), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2022 OpenAI, in the ggml format of
whisper.cpp (https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2023-2024 The ggml authors, copied
unchanged from https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp.
